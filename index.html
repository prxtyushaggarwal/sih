Anyone can report ground movement

A crack in a retaining wall or debris on a road is often the earliest sign a sensor won't catch. Field officials and residents could send a report straight to the nearest control room, with or without a network connection.

GIRIDRISHTI

A landslide inventory and early-warning concept for the North Eastern Region, built for district administrations, field officials and the villages they serve.

Prototype for a Smart India Hackathon submission to the Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region (MDoNER).

The map, hotspot and explorer sections use real historical incident records. The alert log and field-report feed under "Demo" are illustrative mockups of proposed live functionality.
